Buoy Board Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Buoy Board is a reference app that lists NOAA buoy stations, tide stations, NOAA Weather Radio channels and National Weather Service marine forecast zones, with links to the official NOAA and NWS pages.

Information we collect

None. Buoy Board has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information.

Information stored on your device

The stations you pin to My Stations are saved only in your device's local app storage so they are there next time you open the app. They never leave your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes them.

Live buoy readings

To show live readings, the app downloads NOAA's public buoy observation file from a copy on the Buoy Board website, refreshed automatically from NOAA every 15 minutes. The request contains no personal information, and the app keeps the latest readings for your pinned buoys on your device so they can be shown offline.

Links to other websites

When you tap a station or forecast, it opens on a NOAA or National Weather Service website. Those sites have their own privacy practices, which this policy does not cover.

Fonts

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which receives your device's IP address when the fonts download. See Google's privacy policy for details.

Children

Buoy Board does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
